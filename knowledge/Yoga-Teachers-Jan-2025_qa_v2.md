**Swarup Mohdaya - Yoga** _1/21/25, 5:37:29 PM_:
In replying to your question, let me approach it from one step further back. Before even discussing the subject of dharma, we discuss whether human beings are animals. It is at this phase that we introduce the concept of a qualitative vs quantitative difference. And we use it to show that a qualitative difference is sufficient cause for justifying the creation of a separate category of jiiva, whereas a quantitative difference is insufficient cause. So if we are saying that human beings are different because they are "more intelligent" than animals, we cannot justify the creation of a separate category of jiiva i.e. separate jati or kingdom on this basis as there is a whole spectrum of intelligence within the animal kingdom-- and fitting into this spectrum of intelligence in the animal kingdom, if this is all we can say about how humans are different, then we would need to keep humans in the category of animals. 

But that is not the end of the story, right? There is a difference between humans and animals which is not merely quantitative but qualitative. And that means humans have a quality which is not present at all in any animal. Such a difference is sufficient cause for designating a separate category of living beings outside the animal kingdom, known as human beings. Qualitive difference justifies change in jati/kingdom, whereas quantitative difference does not.

Showing that human beings are not animals and why-- this is a critically important point, as it provides a basis for our discussion of dharma as well as for the entire subject of svatantra iccha and "humans in human form" vs "animals in human form".

The reason or spirit behind her question was that you had explained at great length how Prakrti does all the decision-making in the world regarding the extensive work of arranging the fruition of samskara, and as a result it was beginning to seem to her as though the mastermind behind the running of the universe was Prakrti.

So regarding her question as to which is dominant, the reply is: shaktiH sA shivasya shaktiH. Shakti is merely a force, and she is completely obedient, 100% engaged in following the directions she has received from Shiva. Prakrti does not have a mind or brain of her own to be making "decisions" per se. Yes, She does make the arrangements for the fruition of samskara, but that does not involve "decisions" as such; she is merely executing the orders and guidelines she has received from Purusa. Prakrti has received an algorithm from Purusa and She is dedicated for executing it. She is the executor of the algorithm to be sure-- but Purusa is the giver of that algorithm. Purusa is the Master Mind behind everything in the universe. It is similar to the relation between a human being and a computer. It is true that the computer is performing huge work and may appear to be engaged in intricate decision-making all along the way. But actually the computer has no mind and cannot "think" per se. It merely executes what it is designed to do. Similar is the case with Prakrti. She is merely a force; she has no mind and no desires and is simply executing the works she has been ordered to do. The mastermind is Purusa. So Raji ji should understand that Prakrti is not dominant; Purusa is the dominant factor at all times and at all phases of the srsti, as it is His instructions that are being followed.
