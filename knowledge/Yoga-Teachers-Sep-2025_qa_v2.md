So the way to plan and teach these first two sessions is:*
*1. Outline carefully what you are planning to say;*
*2. Ensure that your every sentence supports what you are wanting to present;*
*3. When you stop and ask students to repeat what you have said, ensure that they stay on-track and actually say what it is that you said. *Qualitative Difference vs Quantitative Difference*
1. Don't be confused by the presence of the word "quality" in "qualitative" and its absence in the word "quantitative". Both terms deal directly with qualities/gunas.
2. The difference between these two words is that Qualitative Difference refers to the presence or absence of a quality. If there is a qualitative difference it means the guna is present in one group and absent in the other. Quantitative Difference refers to the situation in which the guna is present in both the groups being compared; what differs is the amount of a guna present, the degree to which it is present.

So the difference in intelligence between animals and humans is a quantitative difference as the quality of intelligence is present in both groups, just to varying degrees. Whereas the difference in "infinite desire" between animals and humans is a qualitative difference, as this this quality of having an "infinite desire" is present in humans and completely absent in animals. 

So presence vs absence of a quality is a qualitative difference; degree of presence of a quality is a quantitative difference. Qualitative difference justifies kingdom change, whereas quantitative difference does not.
